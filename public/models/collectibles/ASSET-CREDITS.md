# Collectible artwork sources

The One Wish Willow paper sleeve uses the packaging net publicly displayed by
the reference website expressly supplied by the user:

- Reference: https://onewishwillow.app/zh/
- Print image: https://onewishwillow.app/ritual/wish-package-paper.png
- Retrieved: 2026-11-13
- Local asset: `wish-package-print.png` (unchanged source image)

The packaging artwork and film-related marks remain the work of their respective
creators. This is a fan display study. Its paper thickness, folds, end flap,
sliding inner tray and willow geometry were separately authored in Blender.

The parquet, brick, textile, rug, patchwork and radar maps are original,
deterministically generated assets. All three collectible meshes were authored
for this repository; reference meshes were not imported.
