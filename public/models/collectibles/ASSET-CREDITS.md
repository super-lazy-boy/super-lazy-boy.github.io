# Collectible artwork sources

The One Wish Willow paper sleeve uses the packaging net publicly displayed by
the reference website expressly supplied by the user:

- Reference: https://onewishwillow.app/zh/
- Print image: https://onewishwillow.app/ritual/wish-package-paper.png
- Retrieved: 2026-11-13
- Local asset: `wish-package-print.png` (unchanged source image)

The packaging artwork and film-related marks remain the work of their respective
creators. This is a fan display study. Its paper thickness, folds, end flap,
sliding inner tray and willow geometry were separately authored in Blender.

The parquet, brick, textile, rug, patchwork and radar maps are original,
deterministically generated assets. All collectible meshes were authored
for this repository; reference meshes were not imported.

Anduril's silhouette, pierced hilt and blade inscription contours were studied
from the user's supplied sword photographs. The inscription is separately
authored vector/mesh geometry traced from that reference; the photographs are
not shipped as textures. The fantasy weapon, decorative tree and franchise marks
belong to their respective creators. This is a fan display study.

The Elder Wand follows the user's supplied photographs and the 15-inch prop
proportions documented by The Noble Collection:
https://noblecollection.com/Item--i-PRP-HP-7145
The six knots, recessed pits, collar, wood colours and display stand are original
Blender geometry. No commercial model, texture or 3D scan was imported.

The black hollow willow prop was rebuilt from the user's latest photographs,
with actual side openings, hollow core and forked ends. The existing source
packaging artwork above is retained unchanged.
