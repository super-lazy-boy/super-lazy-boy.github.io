# TARDIS · 2005–2010 exterior collectible

Original procedural reconstruction approved for the room on 2026-10-21. `geometry.json` contains only construction parameters; no local reference paths or review history is shipped. The 171 physical components and 145401 source vertices match the reviewed standalone model (maximum position difference below 1e-15 in the integration check).

`createTardis.ts` builds the approved geometry and signs, `TardisOptimization.ts` pools repeated geometry, and `TardisSurface.ts` keeps paint distress in the miniature's original coordinate frame. Required estimated surface maps are under `public/media/models/tardis/`; these are source-derived material evidence, not calibrated physical measurements.

Reference collection: https://tardisref.info/tardis-a-b/ and https://thedoctorwhosite.co.uk/tardis/exterior/2005-tardis/ . Doctor Who / TARDIS belong to their respective rights holders.
